# Growth Framework Fixtures

Everything in this package is synthetic. It is a conformance harness, not a second live business and not a source of market, customer or performance data.

`tenant-template` is intentionally incomplete. A real Business B must fill its own BrandPack, ProductTruth, consent/data map, activation event, channel owner, approval owner, hard cap and incident contact in its own private repository. It must never copy another tenant's content, assets, claims, destinations, credentials or customer data.
