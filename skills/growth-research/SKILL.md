# Growth research

Use this skill to prepare a research-to-plan artifact after tenant readiness has passed.

## Inputs

Use only the tenant’s approved source register, sanitized research summaries, BusinessPack, ProductTruth, and accepted research scope. Keep source facts, analyst interpretation, and hypotheses visibly separate.

## Workflow

1. State the decision the research should inform and the smallest question needed to answer it.
2. Inspect source provenance, review status, date, permitted use, and known limitations before drawing an observation.
3. Extract observable needs, contexts, frictions, desired progress, and current alternatives without inferring sensitive traits.
4. Convert observations into falsifiable job-to-be-done hypotheses with confidence, counterevidence, and a proposed test.
5. Identify evidence gaps and record an internal collection plan that does not contact people or handle raw personal data.
6. Produce a research plan that distinguishes facts, hypotheses, assumptions, and decisions still owned by a human.

## Hard stops

Stop if a source lacks a permitted-use status, a summary contains direct identifiers or restricted data, the scope would require outreach, or a hypothesis is being presented as a market fact. Do not import another tenant’s research.

## Human gate

A named owner accepts the research scope and decides whether the hypotheses are sufficiently bounded to inform positioning. The decision does not validate product or comparative claims.

## Validate and hand off

Check that every observation links to an approved source, each hypothesis has a confidence and limitation, and no sensitive targeting conclusion appears. Hand off the accepted hypotheses, evidence gaps, and source references to positioning and journey measurement.
