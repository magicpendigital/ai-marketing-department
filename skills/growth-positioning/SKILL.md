# Growth positioning

Use this skill after research has produced bounded job hypotheses and before content drafting begins.

## Inputs

Require current ProductTruth, approved evidence references, accepted job hypotheses, BrandPack, and a clearly named decision owner. Do not treat a desired message as proof of a product capability.

## Workflow

1. Choose one audience context and one job hypothesis that the evidence can support.
2. Define the relevant current alternative or unmet approach in neutral language.
3. State the product mechanism, allowed proof, value hypothesis, and explicit non-claims.
4. Write one falsifiable experiment brief: audience context, message premise, internal success signal, guardrails, and invalidation condition.
5. Attach evidence references and expiry dates to every product, outcome, or comparison statement.
6. Mark all language as draft and keep the brief inside the tenant boundary.

## Hard stops

Stop if the mechanism is unavailable, evidence is expired or missing, a claim exceeds its proof, or a comparison cannot be supported. Do not select an external audience, make an offer, or imply guaranteed outcomes.

## Human gate

A product or business owner accepts the positioning hypothesis before it can guide a content package. That acceptance does not approve public-facing material.

## Validate and hand off

Verify that the brief distinguishes evidence from assumptions, names an invalidation condition, includes claim limits, and has a current owner. Hand off the versioned brief and evidence list to content creation and quality review.
