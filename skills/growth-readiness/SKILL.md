# Growth readiness

Use this skill at the start of a tenant or when a material change could invalidate its marketing foundation.

## Inputs

Require a completed tenant configuration, BrandPack, BusinessPack, ProductTruth, owner map, consent and data map, risk register, and channel capability record. Treat every missing, expired, or unowned field as unresolved; do not fill it from guesswork.

## Workflow

1. Separate tenant-owned facts from assumptions, desired outcomes, and prohibited claims.
2. Check every intended claim against current ProductTruth evidence, scope, locale, and expiry.
3. Assign a named human owner for product truth, editorial quality, privacy, measurement, and any future execution decision.
4. Record permitted data classes, consent basis, retention boundary, and the aggregate-only rule for learning.
5. Record the current operating mode as draft-only and list workflows that are explicitly deferred.
6. Produce one readiness record with open risks, blockers, required decisions, and the next allowed workflow.

## Hard stops

Stop and return a blocker when an identity boundary, evidence reference, expiry, data/consent map, risk owner, or rollback owner is absent. Also stop if another tenant’s identity, source material, customer data, or approval appears in the pack.

## Human gate

A named owner must accept the ProductTruth scope and readiness record before research or content work starts. Acceptance permits only the next internal workflow; it does not permit external action.

## Validate and hand off

Validate that each claim has an evidence reference and expiry, every risk has an owner and disposition, and the readiness record names its tenant and version. Hand off the record, unresolved items, and allowed-next-workflow statement to the research and measurement owners.
