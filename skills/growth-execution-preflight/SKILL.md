# Growth execution preflight

Use this skill only to document whether a future execution request is complete enough for separate human gate review. In the first release, the related workflows remain deferred.

## Inputs

Require a human-approved execution envelope, current ProductTruth, QA verdict, destination proof, named channel owner, approval state, rollback checklist, and evidence that the material belongs to the correct tenant.

## Workflow

1. Confirm that the request identifies one material version, intended surface, accountable owner, and desired future decision.
2. Recheck claim evidence, QA status, provenance, locale, accessibility, privacy boundary, and approval freshness.
3. Record the minimum future-gate evidence: destination proof, rollback approach, receipt expectation, budget boundary where applicable, and unresolved risk.
4. Identify exactly which separate gate remains required and why the package is not executable now.
5. Produce a preflight checklist whose final state is pending human execution review.

## Hard stops

Stop before channel connection, account access, scheduling, publishing, messaging, audience upload, campaign creation, spend, or optimization. Stop if approval, destination proof, owner, rollback, rights, or evidence is missing or stale.

## Human gate

Every future execution request requires a named human decision at its applicable gate. A completed preflight checklist is evidence for review, never permission to act.

## Validate and hand off

Check the material version, tenant boundary, approval expiry, owner, rollback record, and unresolved-risk list. Hand off the checklist and evidence bundle to the designated human reviewer; do not hand it to an execution mechanism.
